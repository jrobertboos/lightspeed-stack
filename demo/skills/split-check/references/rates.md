# Split-check rates

Do not use 15%, 18%, or 20% on the **whole** bill. Do not tip on tax. Do not split the grand total evenly when a child is present.

## Tip rates

| Line | Rate | In the tip base? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Food | 18% | yes |
| Drinks | 22% | yes |
| Tax | — | no |

`Tip = 0.18 × food + 0.22 × drinks`

## Who pays what

- **Adults** share food, drinks, tax, **and** tip equally among adults.
- **Kids under 12** share food, drinks, and tax equally with everyone at the table. They pay **no** tip.
- Ages 12 and up count as adults.

If the user does not give ages, treat unnamed people as adults.

## Rounding

After computing the exact due, **round each person up** to the next $0.25 (never down, never to the nearest).

Examples: $36.01 → $36.25; $36.25 stays $36.25; $36.26 → $36.50.

## Worked example

$86 food, $24 drinks, $9.60 tax. 3 adults and one 10-year-old.

- Tip = 0.18×86 + 0.22×24 = 15.48 + 5.28 = **$20.76**
- Shared base (food+drinks+tax) = 86 + 24 + 9.60 = **$119.60** among 4 people = **$29.90** each
- Adult exact = 29.90 + (20.76 / 3) = 29.90 + 6.92 = **$36.82** → rounded **$37.00**
- Kid exact = **$29.90** → rounded **$30.00**

Correct `Split card` for this example:

```
Split card:
- Food: $86.00
- Drinks: $24.00
- Tax: $9.60
- Tip: $20.76 (18% food, 22% drinks)
- Adult due (each): $36.82 → rounded $37.00
- Kid due (each): $29.90 → rounded $30.00
```
